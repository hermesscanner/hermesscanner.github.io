Our YouTube app

Our Google / YouTube app signs in only to our own YouTube channel, to schedule our live streams and upload our own videos. It never asks viewers to sign in with Google and collects no viewer’s Google account data. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data, or use it for advertising.
  • Message you first.
  • Ask for exchange keys, passwords, seed phrases or ID documents — anyone who does is not us.
  • Knowingly serve anyone under 18.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, withdraw your consent, nominate someone to act for you, and complain to us and then to the Data Protection Board of India. Write to support.hermesscanner@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

Changes

The date at the top changes when this notice changes; important changes are posted in our Telegram channel.
